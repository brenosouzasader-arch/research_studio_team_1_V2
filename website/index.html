Rooftop solar · Barcelona

Who can use this roof's spare solar?

A big roof often makes more power than its building uses, mostly at weekends and in summer. This tool finds that spare power hour by hour, connects the closest neighbours who can use it, tests whether a battery is worth it with real Barcelona prices, and works out what the roof owner and the neighbours gain.
